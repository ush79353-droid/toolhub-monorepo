## 4. Payroll Calculation — Official Rules

Verify rules for each supported tax year using authoritative sources:

- IRS.
- Social Security Administration.
- Illinois Department of Revenue.
- Relevant official Illinois tax publications.

For the selected year, verify:

- Federal income tax brackets.
- Standard deductions.

- Child/dependent-related credits.

- Medicare rates and applicable additional Medicare thresholds.

Do not invent tax brackets, exemptions or credits.

### AGI Interpretation

AGI is not necessarily gross wages.

Do **not** automatically treat AGI as gross payroll compensation for FICA calculations.

If the screenshot and authorized specification do not supply gross wages or pretax payroll deductions, clearly distinguish:

- Income-tax estimates based on AGI.
- Payroll withholding or FICA calculations requiring gross wages.
- Take-home pay projections requiring additional information.

Do not fabricate exact net pay from missing gross-pay inputs.

### Federal Income Tax

For a supported year:

`Federal Taxable Income = max(0, AGI − Applicable Deduction)`

Use the verified standard deduction or valid itemized deduction treatment.

Apply the official progressive bracket schedule.

Apply only tax credits whose eligibility and value can be reliably calculated from the available inputs.

The number of dependents alone is not sufficient for every federal credit calculation; do not automatically assign unsupported credits.

### Illinois Income Tax

Apply the verified Illinois individual income tax rules for the selected year.

Do not assume federal taxable income equals Illinois taxable income.

Respect Illinois-specific exemptions and adjustments when supported by the available fields.

If the necessary inputs are unavailable, clearly label the result as an estimate and disclose the assumption.

### Pay Frequency

For a valid annual estimate:

`Estimated Tax Per Pay Period = Estimated Annual Tax / Pay Periods Per Year`

Typical counts:

- Weekly: 52.
- Biweekly: 26.
- Semimonthly: 24.
- Monthly: 12.

Use the project's established payroll-period conventions.

Do not imply this annualized estimate equals an employer's exact withholding calculation.

### Post-Tax Deductions

Deduct entered post-tax deductions from estimated net pay only when the relevant period is explicitly established.

Do not subtract post-tax deductions from taxable income.

Prevent negative net-pay displays from being silently represented as valid take-home pay.

## 5. Illinois Property Tax Calculation

Use official Illinois sources where available, including:

- Illinois Department of Revenue property tax reports.
- County assessment/equalization publications.
- Relevant county treasurer or assessor data.

Illinois property taxation depends on assessments, equalization, local taxing districts and exemptions.

A county-wide effective rate is not a parcel-specific levy rate.

For the screenshot's simplified model:

`Estimated Annual Property Tax = Entered Home Value × Verified Effective Property Tax Rate`

**Important:**

- Verify whether the input represents market value or equalized assessed value.
- Do not apply a market-value effective rate directly to an assessed value without the correct interpretation.
- If the screenshot's "Assessed home value" wording conflicts with the sourced rate definition, add a clear explanation rather than silently mixing incompatible bases.
- County-specific rates must have a source year and citation.
- Do not fabricate rates for missing counties.
- Do not treat the screenshot's `1.67%` as proof of Adams County's current rate.
- Explain that actual bills vary by parcel, local taxing districts and exemptions.
- If current reliable county data is unavailable, show an unavailable-rate state instead of an invented percentage.

Do not introduce unsupported full-property-tax filing functionality.
