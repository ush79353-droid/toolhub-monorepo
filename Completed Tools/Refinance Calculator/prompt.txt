## 3. Accurate Refinancing Calculations

Use standard fixed-rate monthly amortization.

- Current monthly rate = Current APR / 1200.
- New monthly rate = New APR / 1200.
- New loan principal = Remaining Balance + Cash-Out Amount + financed costs, if explicitly selected.
- Points cost = Applicable loan principal × Points / 100.
- Monthly payment = Principal × Rate / [1 − (1 + Rate)^(-Months)].
- At 0% APR, monthly payment = Principal / Months.

Requirements:
- Determine the remaining current-loan term from balance, payment and rate using a stable logarithmic solution or amortization schedule.
- Calculate new loan interest, monthly payment and total repayment.
- Distinguish upfront fees from financed fees; never double-count costs.
- Include cash-out proceeds separately when reporting economic savings.
- Calculate break-even using an appropriate cumulative cash-flow method; identify when no break-even exists.
- Distinguish monthly savings from lifetime savings, especially when loan terms differ.
- Validate insufficient monthly payments, zero rates and invalid inputs.
- Preserve full precision and round only for display.
- Do not invent unsupported tax benefits or refinancing products.
