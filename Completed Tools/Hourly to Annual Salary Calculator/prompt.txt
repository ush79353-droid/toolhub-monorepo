## 3) Required Calculator Logic

Use these formulas:

- **Weekly pay** = Hourly wage × Hours per week
- **Annual salary** = Weekly pay × 52
- **Monthly pay** = Annual salary ÷ 12
- **Per day** = Weekly pay ÷ Days per week

### Validation rules
- Hourly wage must be **>= 0**
- Hours per week must be **> 0**
- Days per week must be **> 0**
- Prevent divide-by-zero, NaN, Infinity
- Keep internal precision high; round only for display
- Do **not** add tax, overtime, deductions, holidays, or unsupported assumptions
- Results should update instantly when inputs change

### Notes / assumptions
- Use **52 weeks per year**
- Daily pay is based on **Days per week** input
- Show clean helper text / tooltip if needed for assumptions
