## 3. Accurate Calculations

For tax rate `r` as a percentage of gross:

**Gross to Net**
- Tax = Gross × r / 100.
- Net = Gross − Tax.

**Net to Gross**
- Gross = Net / (1 − r/100).
- Tax = Gross − Net.

Requirements:
- Clearly state that tax is deducted as a percentage of gross, not added as VAT.
- Validate nonnegative monetary amounts.
- Validate tax between 0% and 100%.
- In net-to-gross mode, tax must be below 100% for a defined reverse calculation, except that a zero-net/100%-tax case is indeterminate and must not produce a fabricated gross.
- Support zero amounts and zero tax.
- Recalculate when mode or inputs change.
- Prevent NaN, Infinity and circular updates.
- Preserve full precision; round only for display.
- Do not invent progressive income tax brackets or deductions.
