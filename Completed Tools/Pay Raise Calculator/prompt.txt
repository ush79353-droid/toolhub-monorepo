### 4. Pay Period Conversion Rules

Let:

- `H` = hours worked per week.
- `W = 52` weeks per year.

Annualize compensation using:

`Annual Pay = Hourly Pay × H × 52`

`Weekly Pay = Annual Pay / 52`

`Monthly Pay = Annual Pay / 12`

`Hourly Pay = Annual Pay / (H × 52)`

Use the same normalization for all monetary raise amounts.

Do not approximate a month as exactly four weeks.

Preserve full numerical precision.

### 5. Percentage Raise Calculation

Let:

- `P` = original annual pay.
- `r` = raise percentage.

Calculate:

`Annual Raise = P × r / 100`

`New Annual Pay = P + Annual Raise`

Derive hourly, weekly and monthly raise amounts from Annual Raise.

Derive the four after-raise pay fields from New Annual Pay.

**Example:**

Original hourly pay: 1,000 PKR

Hours per week: 40

Raise: 10%

- Weekly pay before raise = 40,000 PKR.
- Annual pay before raise = 2,080,000 PKR.
- Annual raise = 208,000 PKR.
- Annual pay after raise = 2,288,000 PKR.
- Hourly pay after raise = 1,100 PKR.

### 6. Monetary Raise Calculation

If the user enters a monetary raise amount in any supported period:

1. Identify the monetary raise input most recently edited.
2. Normalize it to the canonical calculation currency.
3. Convert its period to an annual raise amount.
4. Derive corresponding hourly, weekly, monthly and annual raise values.
5. Calculate the raise percentage when original annual pay is positive.

Formula:

`Raise Percentage = (Annual Raise / Original Annual Pay) × 100`

`New Annual Pay = Original Annual Pay + Annual Raise`

When original pay is zero:

- A positive monetary raise produces an undefined percentage increase.
- Do not display Infinity or fabricate 0%.
- Clearly show that the percentage is undefined.
- A zero original pay and zero raise may display an agreed zero-change state with explanation.

Handle monetary raise values and percentage changes consistently.
