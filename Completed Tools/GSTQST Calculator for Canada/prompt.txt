## 5. Accurate Tax Calculations

### GST-only Provinces and Territories

`GST Amount = Amount Before Tax × GST Rate`

`Amount After Tax = Amount Before Tax + GST Amount`

### HST Provinces

`HST Amount = Amount Before Tax × Applicable HST Rate`

`Amount After Tax = Amount Before Tax + HST Amount`

HST is a combined tax; do not add GST again on top of an HST rate.

### GST + Provincial Sales Tax

Where applicable:

`GST Amount = Amount Before Tax × GST Rate`

`PST Amount = Amount Before Tax × Provincial Rate`

`Amount After Tax = Amount Before Tax + GST Amount + PST Amount`

Verify the correct provincial tax base and any relevant tax-on-tax exceptions before calculating.

### Quebec GST + QST

For ordinary taxable supplies under the general current rules:

`GST Amount = Amount Before Tax × GST Rate`

`QST Amount = Amount Before Tax × QST Rate`

`Amount After Tax = Amount Before Tax + GST Amount + QST Amount`

Do not incorrectly calculate QST on a GST-inclusive amount unless authoritative rules for the specified transaction require that method.
