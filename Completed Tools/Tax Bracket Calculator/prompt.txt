### 5. Progressive Tax Calculation

Let:

- `I` = confirmed taxable income.
- `D` = deductible amount, only when legally and semantically applicable.
- `T` = final taxable income used for bracket calculation.

If the input is already taxable income:

`T = max(0, I)`

If the authorized specification explicitly treats it as income before deductions:

`T = max(0, I − D)`

Apply progressive brackets.

For each bracket:

`Tax in Bracket = Taxable Portion in Bracket × Applicable Marginal Rate`

Then:

`Total Federal Tax = Sum of Tax Across All Applicable Brackets`

Do not multiply all taxable income by the highest marginal rate.

### 6. Marginal and Effective Tax Rates

Calculate:

**Marginal Tax Rate**

The rate applicable to the next taxable dollar under the selected bracket schedule.

**Effective Tax Rate**

`Effective Tax Rate = Total Estimated Federal Income Tax / Taxable Income × 100`

Handle zero taxable income safely.

Where useful, display:

- Taxable income.
- Estimated federal income tax.
- Marginal tax bracket.
- Effective tax rate.
- Tax breakdown by bracket.

Do not label a tax-bracket estimate as a complete federal tax-return liability.
