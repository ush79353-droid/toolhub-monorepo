## 3. Accurate Lease Calculations

Use a standard money-factor lease model:

- Residual value = MSRP × Residual Factor / 100.
- Net capitalized cost = Negotiated Selling Price + Dealer Fee + Registration Fee − Down Payment − Trade-in Value − Other Reductions.
- Monthly depreciation = (Net Capitalized Cost − Residual Value) / Lease Months.
- Monthly lease fee = (Net Capitalized Cost + Residual Value) × Money Factor.
- Monthly base payment = Monthly Depreciation + Monthly Lease Fee.
- Monthly sales tax = Monthly Base Payment × Sales Tax / 100.
- Monthly lease payment = Monthly Base Payment + Monthly Sales Tax.

Requirements:
- Clearly disclose that fees are assumed financed into capitalized cost and sales tax is applied monthly.
- Avoid counting down payment, trade-in or other reductions twice.
- Validate positive term, nonnegative fees and valid residual factor.
- Handle zero money factor, zero tax and zero down payment.
- Prevent negative or nonfinite payments; report unsupported cases instead of silently clamping results.
- Preserve full precision and round only for display.
- Do not invent additional taxes or lease penalties.
