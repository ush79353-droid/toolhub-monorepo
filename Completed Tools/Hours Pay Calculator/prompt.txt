## 4. Accurate Pay Calculations

Normalize every pay value into a common annual amount and calculate the remaining periods.

Let:

- `H` = working hours per day.
- `D` = working days per week.
- `W` = working weeks per year, default 52.
- `Weekly Hours = H × D`
- `Annual Hours = H × D × W`

### From Daily Pay

`Weekly Pay = Daily Pay × D`

`Annual Pay = Daily Pay × D × W`

`Monthly Pay = Annual Pay / 12`

`Hourly Wage = Daily Pay / H`

### From Weekly Pay

`Daily Pay = Weekly Pay / D`

`Annual Pay = Weekly Pay × W`

`Monthly Pay = Annual Pay / 12`

`Hourly Wage = Weekly Pay / (H × D)`

### From Monthly Pay

`Annual Pay = Monthly Pay × 12`

`Weekly Pay = Annual Pay / W`

`Daily Pay = Annual Pay / (W × D)`

`Hourly Wage = Annual Pay / (H × D × W)`

### From Annual Pay

`Monthly Pay = Annual Pay / 12`

`Weekly Pay = Annual Pay / W`

`Daily Pay = Annual Pay / (W × D)`

`Hourly Wage = Annual Pay / (H × D × W)`

These are standard time-based pay conversions, not country-specific statutory payroll calculations.
