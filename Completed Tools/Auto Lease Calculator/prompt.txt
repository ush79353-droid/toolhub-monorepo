## 3. Accurate Lease Calculations

Use the standard money-factor lease model:

- Adjusted capitalized cost = Auto price − Down payment − Trade-in value.
- Monthly depreciation = (Adjusted capitalized cost − Residual value) / Lease months.
- Monthly finance charge = (Adjusted capitalized cost + Residual value) × Money factor.
- Base monthly payment = Depreciation + Finance charge.
- Monthly payment including tax = Base monthly payment × (1 + Sales tax / 100), when monthly-payment tax treatment applies.
- Total lease installments = Monthly payment × Lease months.

Requirements:
- Clearly document tax assumptions; sales tax treatment varies by jurisdiction.
- If APR is selected, use the conventional approximate conversion `Money Factor = APR (%) / 2400`, with appropriate disclosure.
- Avoid double-counting down payments, trade-in allowances and upfront taxes.
- Validate residual, term, down payment and trade-in amounts.
- Handle zero interest, zero tax and zero residual.
- Preserve precision; round only for display.
- Do not invent unsupported acquisition fees, registration costs or disposition fees.
