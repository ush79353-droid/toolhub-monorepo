## 3. Debt Avalanche Calculation

Implement accurate multiple-credit-card repayment using the **Debt Avalanche Method**:

1. Calculate monthly interest for each outstanding card using its APR.
2. Pay the required minimum on every active card.
3. Allocate the remaining monthly budget to the card with the highest APR.
4. Once a card is paid off, redirect its available payment allocation to the next highest-APR card.
5. Continue until every card balance reaches zero.

Requirements:
- Never exceed the specified monthly budget.
- Cap payments at each card's remaining balance plus accrued interest.
- Validate positive balances, nonnegative APRs and minimum payments.
- Detect when the budget cannot cover required minimums.
- Detect non-amortizing repayment scenarios.
- Handle zero-interest cards, equal APRs and partial final payments.
- Preserve full precision and round only for display.
- Prevent infinite loops and unrealistic schedules.
- Explain the monthly interest-accrual assumption.

Reuse the existing Debt Avalanche engine if its behavior matches these requirements.
