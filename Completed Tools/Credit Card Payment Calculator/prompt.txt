## 3. Accurate Calculation Logic

Calculate the monthly payment required to repay credit-card debt within the specified term.

For standard monthly compounding:

`Monthly Rate = APR / 1200`

`Number of Payments = Years × 12 + Months`

`Monthly Payment = Balance × Rate / (1 − (1 + Rate)^(-Number of Payments))`

Requirements:
- For 0% APR, monthly payment = balance / number of payments.
- Calculate total payments and interest using a repayment schedule.
