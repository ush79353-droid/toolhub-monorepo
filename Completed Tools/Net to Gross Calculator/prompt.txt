### 4. Mathematical Definitions

Let:

- `N` = net amount, before tax.
- `G` = gross amount, including tax.
- `T` = tax amount.
- `rN` = percentage tax calculated from net.
- `rG` = percentage tax calculated from gross.

Fundamental relationship:

`G = N + T`

Tax rates are two representations of the same tax.

**Percentage From Net**

`rN = (T / N) × 100`

**Percentage From Gross**

`rG = (T / G) × 100`

Do not treat `rN` and `rG` as two separate taxes.

---

### 5. Calculation From Net Amount and Net-Based Tax Rate

When Net Amount and Percentage Tax (From Net) are the authoritative inputs:

`Tax Amount = N × rN / 100`

`Gross Amount = N + Tax Amount`

`Percentage Tax (From Gross) = (Tax Amount / Gross Amount) × 100`

Equivalent rate relationship:

`rG = 100 × rN / (100 + rN)`

Example:

Net amount = 100

Net-based tax = 20%

Tax amount = 20

Gross amount = 120

Gross-based tax = 16.666666...%

Do not incorrectly display both tax rates as 20%.

---

### 6. Calculation From Gross Amount and Gross-Based Tax Rate

When Gross Amount and Percentage Tax (From Gross) are authoritative:

`Tax Amount = G × rG / 100`

`Net Amount = G − Tax Amount`

`Percentage Tax (From Net) = (Tax Amount / Net Amount) × 100`

Equivalent rate relationship:

`rN = 100 × rG / (100 − rG)`

Example:

Gross amount = 120

Gross-based tax = 16.666666...%

Tax amount = 20

Net amount = 100

Net-based tax = 20%.

For nonnegative net and tax, require:

`0 ≤ rG < 100`

Handle the 100% boundary safely rather than dividing by zero.

### 7. Reverse Calculations

Support the additional mathematically valid combinations required by the authorized calculator specification.

#### Net Amount + Gross-Based Tax Rate

`Gross = Net / (1 − rG/100)`

`Tax = Gross − Net`

#### Gross Amount + Net-Based Tax Rate

`Net = Gross / (1 + rN/100)`

`Tax = Gross − Net`

#### Net Amount + Tax Amount

`Gross = Net + Tax`

`rN = Tax / Net × 100`

`rG = Tax / Gross × 100`

#### Gross Amount + Tax Amount

`Net = Gross − Tax`

`rN = Tax / Net × 100`

`rG = Tax / Gross × 100`

#### Net Amount + Gross Amount

`Tax = Gross − Net`

Derive both tax percentages from the resulting amounts.

Validate `Gross ≥ Net` when modeling nonnegative tax.

If the user provides insufficient or contradictory independent values, do not fabricate a solution. Clearly explain which additional value is required.
