## 3. Accurate GST Calculations

Treat **gross price as GST-inclusive** and **net price as GST-exclusive**.

For GST rate `r`:

**From Net Price**
- `Gross = Net × (1 + r/100)`
- `Tax Amount = Gross − Net`

**From Gross Price**
- `Net = Gross / (1 + r/100)`
- `Tax Amount = Gross − Net`

Requirements:
- Clearly distinguish tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive prices.
- Support zero GST with Gross = Net and Tax = 0.
- Validate nonnegative monetary values.
- Validate GST rate ≥ 0 and a sensible project-supported upper bound.
- Prevent circular calculations when switching the authoritative field.
- Prevent NaN, Infinity and division by zero.
- Preserve full numerical precision; round only for display.
- Do not invent country-specific GST rates, exemptions or tax brackets.
- Do not automatically apply an unverified default GST percentage.
