## 4. Calculation Logic

### Core formula

Amount earned from selling leave:

`Leave Payout = Daily Base Pay × Leave Days to Sell`

The daily base pay depends on the selected salary payment frequency.

**Important policy requirement:** A legally enforceable leave-encashment formula is not universal. Inspect existing project salary/leave utilities and the authorized task specification for a defined divisor or employment-policy rule.

If no specific policy is established, implement a clearly labeled **working-day estimate**, not a statutory or guaranteed leave payout.

Use these explicit estimate assumptions:

- 52 paid weeks per year.
- 5 paid working days per week.
- 8 paid hours per working day.
- 260 working days per year.

### Salary conversion formulas

**Annual**

`Daily Pay = Annual Salary / 260`

**Monthly**

`Daily Pay = (Monthly Salary × 12) / 260`

**Weekly**

`Daily Pay = Weekly Salary / 5`

**Hourly**

`Daily Pay = Hourly Rate × 8`

**Final**

`Leave Payout = Daily Pay × Leave Days`

### Example — Monthly mode

Base pay: PKR 60,000/month

Leave days to sell: 5

Annual salary = 60,000 × 12 = PKR 720,000

Daily pay = 720,000 / 260 = PKR 2,769.230769...

Leave payout = 2,769.230769... × 5

**Expected result: PKR 13,846.15**

Avoid premature rounding.

If the verified project's existing approved leave-encashment policy uses a different divisor, follow that authoritative rule instead and explain the resulting method.

### Calculation limitations

- Do not automatically deduct income tax, payroll deductions, or fees.
- Do not claim an employee is legally entitled to cash out leave.
- Do not confuse working-day-based estimates with a 30-calendar-day monthly salary divisor.
- Make the chosen basis visible in a short calculation explanation or existing guide content.
- Do not add employer-specific policy controls unless authorized.
