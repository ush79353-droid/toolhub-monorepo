## 5. Calculation Methodology

The conceptual starting point is:

`MAGI = AGI + Applicable Add-Backs ± Applicable Adjustments`

However, the exact adjustments depend on the selected or established tax purpose.

### AGI

Treat AGI as an already-calculated value.

Do not automatically reconstruct AGI from other fields.

### Foreign-Earned Income

Determine whether the specific MAGI definition requires adding back excluded foreign-earned income.

Do not automatically add all foreign-earned wages if they were already included in AGI.

### Foreign Housing Cost

Distinguish applicable foreign housing exclusions/deductions from general housing expenses.

Only include relevant amounts according to the verified MAGI definition.

### Non-Taxable Social Benefits

Identify what the field represents.

For relevant definitions, nontaxable Social Security benefits may be included in MAGI.

Do not treat all social benefits as interchangeable.

Clearly state the interpretation used.

### Self-Employment Taxes

Do not automatically add the entire self-employment tax liability.

Determine whether the applicable MAGI calculation adds back the deductible portion of self-employment tax or another specific adjustment.

If the field's meaning is ambiguous, document the interpretation rather than silently assuming the full amount qualifies.

### IRA Contributions

Only include IRA-related deductions or adjustments where required by the verified MAGI definition.

Distinguish deductible traditional IRA contributions from Roth or nondeductible contributions.

Do not automatically add back all retirement savings.

### Passive Income or Losses

Preserve the correct sign and applicable tax treatment.

Do not automatically add back every passive-income amount.

Review applicable restrictions and limitations for passive activity losses.

### Other Adjustments

Treat the Others field as a clearly documented user-supplied adjustment.

Validate its sign and prevent unsupported automatic tax claims.

### Employer-Paid Adoption Benefits

Determine whether excluded employer-provided adoption benefits must be added back under the established MAGI definition.

Do not automatically apply adoption-related rules to unrelated MAGI purposes.

### Savings Bond Interest

Distinguish tax-exempt savings bond interest from taxable interest already included in AGI.

Avoid double-counting.

### Student Loan Interest

Add back a student loan interest deduction only when the established MAGI definition requires it.

Do not assume the entire paid interest amount was deducted.

### Qualified Tuition Expenses and Fees

Check the effective-year legal treatment of tuition-related deductions or exclusions.

Do not treat an expired deduction as currently active.

Do not confuse education tax credits with income deductions.
