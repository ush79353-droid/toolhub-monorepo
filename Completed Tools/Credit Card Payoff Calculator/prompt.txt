- APR
- fixed monthly payment
Implement accurate payoff logic:

- monthlyRate = APR / 100 / 12
- If APR = 0:
  months = balance / monthlyPayment
- Otherwise, calculate amortized payoff month-by-month until balance reaches 0.
Requirements

  - principalPaid = monthlyPayment - interestForMonth

- Continue until balance is fully paid.

- Compute:
  - total months
  - breakdown into years / months / days
  - total paid
- For partial final month/day estimate, use reasonable prorated daily approximation based on monthly balance payoff.
- Preserve calculation precision internally; round only for display.
