## 3. Accurate Calculations

Use 52 paid weeks annually and 26 biweekly periods.

- From annual salary: `Biweekly = Annual Salary / 26`
- From monthly wage: `Biweekly = Monthly Wage × 12 / 26`
- From weekly wage: `Biweekly = Weekly Wage × 2`
- From daily wage: `Biweekly = Daily Wage × Days Per Week × 2`
- From hourly wage: `Biweekly = Hourly Wage × Hours Per Week × 2`

Requirements:
- Biweekly means every two weeks, not twice per month.
- Validate nonnegative wages.
- Validate positive working hours and working days when required.
- Support zero wages.
- Avoid circular calculations and conflicting inputs.
- Prevent NaN, Infinity and division by zero.
- Preserve full precision; round only for display.
- Do not invent tax, overtime or deduction fields.
