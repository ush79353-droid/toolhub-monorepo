- Handle ZIP codes spanning multiple jurisdictions safely.

- Implement Florida's applicable discretionary surtax rules, including the **$5,000 limitation on a single taxable item where applicable**, only when sufficient information about the transaction exists.
- Do not automatically apply the surtax cap to an aggregate purchase without item-level information.
- Never invent tax rates, effective dates or product exemptions.

Basic formulas:

`State Tax = Taxable Amount × Applicable State Rate`

`County Surtax = Applicable Surtax Base × Verified County Rate`

`Total Tax = State Tax + County Surtax`

`Total Price = Purchase Amount + Total Tax`

Treat the purchase as a taxable general retail transaction unless reliable transaction details indicate otherwise.

If accurate ZIP-based tax rates or required surtax treatment cannot be determined, show a clear limitation instead of a fabricated total.
