### 4. Accurate Monthly to Hourly Conversion

Use calendar-year average normalization.

Let:

- `M` = monthly income.
- `H` = working hours per week.
- `W = 52` weeks per year.
- `Months = 12`.

Calculate:

`Annual Income = M × 12`

`Annual Working Hours = H × 52`

`Hourly Income = (M × 12) / (H × 52)`

Equivalent:

`Hourly Income = Monthly Income / (Hours Per Week × 52 / 12)`

Do not use an inaccurate four-weeks-per-month assumption.

**Example:**

Monthly income: 100,000 PKR

Hours per week: 40

`Hourly Income = (100,000 × 12) / (40 × 52)`

`Hourly Income ≈ 576.92 PKR/hour`

Keep full numerical precision internally.

Round only for display.
