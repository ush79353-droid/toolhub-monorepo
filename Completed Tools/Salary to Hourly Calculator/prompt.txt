## 3. Accurate Calculations

Use consistent annualization assumptions:

- Annual working hours = Hours per week × 52.
- Annual salary = Hourly wage × Annual working hours.
- Hourly wage = Annual salary / Annual working hours.
- Weekly wage = Annual salary / 52.
- Biweekly wage = Annual salary / 26.
- Monthly wage = Annual salary / 12.
- Daily wage = Weekly wage / 5, assuming a five-day workweek.

Requirements:
- Explain the 52-week and five-day assumptions.
- Validate positive working hours and nonnegative wages.
- Support zero salary and zero hourly wage.
- Preserve full precision; round only for display.
- Prevent NaN, Infinity, division by zero and circular calculations.
