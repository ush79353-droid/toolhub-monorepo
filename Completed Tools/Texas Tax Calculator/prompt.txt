## 5. Texas Income-Tax Rules

### Fundamental Texas rule

**Texas has no state personal income tax.**

Therefore, the standard Texas state individual income-tax amount is:

`Texas State Individual Income Tax = $0`

Do not implement:

- Fictional Texas state income-tax brackets.
- Fictional Texas personal-income-tax deductions.
- An assumed Texas flat income-tax rate.
- Another state's income-tax rules.

Do not confuse Texas sales tax, franchise tax, or local property taxes with state individual income tax.

Texas users may still be subject to:

- Federal individual income tax.
- Federal payroll taxes where applicable.
- Local property taxes when they own taxable property.
- Other specific taxes outside this calculator's approved scope.

Only calculate tax types supported by the verified tool specification.

## 6. Federal Income-Tax Calculation

Use official IRS rules for the selected tax year.

### Official federal reference

IRS:

https://www.irs.gov/

2026 federal inflation adjustments:

https://www.irs.gov/irb/2025-45_IRB

Verify the currently applicable official 2026 tax-year rules before implementing the final calculation tables.

### Calculation workflow

**Step 1 — Normalize AGI**

Convert the entered AGI into an annual value using the selected income period.

**Step 2 — Determine allowable deductions**

Apply the correct standard deduction or authorized itemized deduction treatment.

**Step 3 — Calculate taxable income**

For a simplified model where all required assumptions are satisfied:

`Federal Taxable Income = max(0, Annual AGI − Applicable Deduction)`

Include further adjustments only where required by verified tax rules and supported by available inputs.

**Step 4 — Apply progressive federal brackets**

Use the official IRS bracket thresholds for:

- Selected tax year.
- Selected filing status.

Calculate each bracket portion progressively.

Do not multiply the entire taxable income by its highest marginal bracket rate.

**Step 5 — Apply supported credits**

Apply only credits whose eligibility and amount can be determined from the verified specification and available inputs.

**Step 6 — Calculate estimated federal tax**

`Estimated Federal Income Tax = Applicable Federal Tax Before Credits − Supported Tax Credits`

Apply relevant statutory limitations and floors.

Do not present this simplified result as an exact filed tax return when additional tax factors are unmodeled.

### Calculation requirements

- Use verified 2026 federal tax brackets.
- Use verified 2026 standard deductions.
- Use the correct filing-status table.
- Distinguish effective and marginal tax rates.
- Handle zero taxable income.
- Handle high incomes and applicable limitations.
- Preserve internal precision.
- Do not fabricate unsupported credits.
- Do not incorrectly apply payroll taxes to AGI.

### Simplified effective-rate estimate

`Estimated Annual Property Tax = Applicable Property Value × Effective Property Tax Rate / 100`

- Assessed/taxable value under the simplified model: $200,000

### Property tax and paycheck handling
