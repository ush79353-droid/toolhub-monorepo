## 5. Accurate Mileage Calculations

Let:

- `B` = business distance in miles.
- `M` = medical distance in miles.
- `C` = charitable distance in miles.
- `Rb` = verified business rate per mile.
- `Rm` = verified medical rate per mile.
- `Rc` = verified charitable rate per mile.

Calculate:

`Business Amount = B × Rb`

`Medical Amount = M × Rm`

`Charitable Amount = C × Rc`

`Total Mileage Deduction = Business Amount + Medical Amount + Charitable Amount`

All rates must be expressed in USD per mile.

### Kilometer Conversion

For kilometer inputs:

`Miles = Kilometers / 1.609344`

Use the precise international conversion factor.

Maintain the same underlying physical distance when the user changes a unit selector.

Do not reinterpret 100 kilometers as 100 miles.

## 6. Tax Deduction vs Reimbursement

The calculator title includes "Reimbursement," while the screenshot labels the output "Mileage deduction."

These concepts must be distinguished.

- IRS standard mileage rates may be used for eligible mileage deduction calculations.
- Employer reimbursement policies may differ from IRS standard rates.
- Medical and charitable mileage deductions depend on applicable tax rules and eligibility.
- Adding all three calculated amounts provides a **nominal combined mileage amount**, not necessarily the amount deductible on a federal tax return.

Clearly disclose these distinctions in supporting text.

Do not claim every business, medical or charitable trip is automatically deductible.

Business mileage eligibility may depend on employment/self-employment circumstances and applicable law.

Medical and charitable deductions may be subject to additional federal tax rules.

Do not calculate tax savings by multiplying the mileage amount by an invented income tax bracket.
