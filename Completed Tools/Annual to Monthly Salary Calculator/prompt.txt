## 3. Accurate Calculations

Assume 52 paid weeks annually and a five-day working week:

- Monthly salary = Annual salary / 12.
- Weekly salary = Annual salary / 52.
- Daily salary = Annual salary / (52 × 5).
- Hourly salary = Annual salary / (52 × Hours per week).
