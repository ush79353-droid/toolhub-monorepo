## 3. Accurate Calculation Logic

Implement source-dependent formulas.

Let:
- D&A = Depreciation and amortization
- T = Corporate tax rate (decimal)
- CapEx = Fixed capital investment
- ΔNWC = Working capital investment

**Net Income source:**

`FCFF = NI + D&A + Interest × (1 − T) − CapEx − ΔNWC`

**EBITDA source:**

`FCFF = EBITDA × (1 − T) + D&A × T − CapEx − ΔNWC`

**EBIT source:**

`FCFF = EBIT × (1 − T) + D&A − CapEx − ΔNWC`

**CFO source:**

`FCFF = CFO + Interest × (1 − T) − CapEx`

For CFO mode, treat CFO as operating cash flow after working-capital movements; do not deduct ΔNWC twice.

- Define CapEx and ΔNWC as positive cash outflows.
- Allow negative FCFF and negative working-capital investment.
- Prevent NaN and Infinity.
- Preserve internal precision; round only for display.
- Explain source-specific assumptions clearly.
