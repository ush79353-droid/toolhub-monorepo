## 3. Accurate Sales Tax Logic

- Resolve the ZIP code against verified California sales tax jurisdiction data.
- Use the applicable combined state, county, city and district sales tax rate.
- Prefer official California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA) data or an existing authorized project tax-rate service.
- **ZIP codes may span multiple tax jurisdictions.** If ZIP alone cannot identify an exact rate, clearly show the ambiguity and request a more precise location instead of inventing a rate.
- Never assume all California locations have the same tax rate.
- Never hardcode an unverified statewide or local tax rate.

Calculate:

`Sales Tax = Purchase Amount × Applicable Tax Rate / 100`

`Total Price = Purchase Amount + Sales Tax`

Validate nonnegative purchase amounts and valid ZIP codes. Preserve precision and apply appropriate display rounding.

Treat the result as an estimate for a fully taxable purchase unless verified product-specific exemptions are supported.
