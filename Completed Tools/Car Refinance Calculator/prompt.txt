## 3. Accurate Calculations

Use standard fixed-rate amortization:

`Monthly Rate = Annual Rate / 1200`

`Monthly Payment = Principal × Monthly Rate / [1 − (1 + Monthly Rate)^(-Months)]`

For 0% interest:

`Monthly Payment = Principal / Months`
