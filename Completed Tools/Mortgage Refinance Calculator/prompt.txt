## 3. Accurate Refinancing Calculations

Use fixed-rate amortization formulas:

`Payment = Principal × Monthly Rate / [1 − (1 + Monthly Rate)^(-Months)]`

For zero interest:

`Payment = Principal / Months`

Requirements:
- Calculate outstanding principal from original mortgage amount, rate, term and elapsed payment periods.
- Use current and new mortgage dates consistently.
- Support selected compounding frequencies by converting nominal annual interest into the effective monthly rate.
- Apply mortgage points and refinancing fees correctly.
- Distinguish financed expenses from upfront cash costs.
- Handle cash-out as additional borrowing and cash-in as principal reduction.
- Calculate remaining old-loan interest and new-loan interest separately.
- Compare total payments over relevant loan terms without misleading savings claims.
- Calculate break-even from actual monthly cash-flow differences and upfront refinancing costs.
- Detect cases where refinancing never breaks even.
- Prevent negative balances, division by zero and invalid date ranges.
- Preserve full precision; round only for display.
- Do not invent taxes, insurance or unsupported mortgage features.
