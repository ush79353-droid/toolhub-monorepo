FUNCTIONAL SCOPE, DEFAULTS, RESULTS AND TOOL-SPECIFIC CHECKS

Visible form and defaults
Blank Age in years and Number of frozen mature eggs. No output is captured. Label age at freezing if the approved model uses that age. Verify any revealed result fields before adding them.

Engine and acceptance
Use a specifically approved published outcome model with its original population, age bounds, mature-oocyte definition, and endpoint. Goldman et al. (Hum Reprod 2017, doi:10.1093/humrep/dex008) is a candidate for a modeled chance of at least one live birth, not proof of the reference implementation. Verify all coefficients or source tables, assumptions for survival/fertilization/euploidy/transfer and rounding. Use P(at least one) = 1−(1−p)^n only if the selected publication defines identical independent trials with that p. Never infer an egg target or guarantee a birth. Use integer age/count per model, handle zero safely, suppress out-of-domain values, and test published cases and result bounds.
