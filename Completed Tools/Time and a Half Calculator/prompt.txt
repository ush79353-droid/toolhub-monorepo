### 4. Accurate Calculation Formulas

Let:

- `R` = standard hourly rate.
- `Hs` = standard hours.
- `H15` = time-and-a-half hours.
- `H2` = double-time hours.
- `H3` = triple-time hours.

**Hourly Rates**

`Time-and-Half Rate = R × 1.5`

`Double-Time Rate = R × 2`

`Triple-Time Rate = R × 3`

**Category Earnings**

`Standard Pay = R × Hs`

`Time-and-Half Pay = (R × 1.5) × H15`

`Double-Time Pay = (R × 2) × H2`

`Triple-Time Pay = (R × 3) × H3`

**Total Earnings**

`Total Pay = Standard Pay + Time-and-Half Pay + Double-Time Pay + Triple-Time Pay`

Example:

- Standard hourly rate = 1,000 PKR.
- Standard hours = 40.
- Time-and-a-half hours = 5.
- Double-time hours = 2.
- Triple-time hours = 0.

Results:

- Standard pay = 40,000 PKR.
- Time-and-a-half pay = 7,500 PKR.
- Double-time pay = 4,000 PKR.
- Triple-time pay = 0 PKR.
- **Total pay = 51,500 PKR.**

Do not count premium hours again under standard hours.
