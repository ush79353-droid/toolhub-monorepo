### 4. Accurate Calculation Model

Normalize calculations using:

- `H` = working hours per day.
- `D` = working days per week.
- `W` = working weeks per year.

Default values:

`H = 8`

`D = 5`

`W = 52`

Derived values:

`Weekly Working Hours = H × D`

`Annual Working Hours = H × D × W`

`Annual Working Days = D × W`

`Average Working Weeks Per Month = W / 12`

These are working-time assumptions used to convert income periods.

#### From Hourly Earnings

If Hourly Pay is the authoritative input:

`Daily Pay = Hourly Pay × H`

`Weekly Pay = Hourly Pay × H × D`

`Yearly Pay = Hourly Pay × H × D × W`

`Monthly Income = Yearly Pay / 12`

#### From Daily Earnings

If Daily Pay is authoritative:

`Hourly Pay = Daily Pay / H`

`Weekly Pay = Daily Pay × D`

`Yearly Pay = Daily Pay × D × W`

`Monthly Income = Yearly Pay / 12`

#### From Weekly Earnings

If Weekly Pay is authoritative:

`Hourly Pay = Weekly Pay / (H × D)`

`Daily Pay = Weekly Pay / D`

`Yearly Pay = Weekly Pay × W`

`Monthly Income = Yearly Pay / 12`

#### From Yearly Earnings

If Yearly Pay is authoritative:

`Hourly Pay = Yearly Pay / (H × D × W)`

`Daily Pay = Yearly Pay / (D × W)`

`Weekly Pay = Yearly Pay / W`

`Monthly Income = Yearly Pay / 12`

Do not use approximate four-weeks-per-month conversion.

Use annual normalization to avoid inconsistent monthly earnings.

- Reveal the verified working-time inputs.
- Validate each field.
- Recalculate derived earnings and monthly income immediately.
- Preserve the original earnings source value.
- Preserve the source currency.

- Reject invalid, nonfinite or zero denominators.
