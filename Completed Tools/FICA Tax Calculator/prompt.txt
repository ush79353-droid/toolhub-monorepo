## 3. Accurate FICA Calculations

Use authoritative IRS and SSA rules for the selected tax year.

**Employee/Employer Mode**
- Employee Social Security tax = Applicable covered wages × 6.2%, subject to the annual Social Security wage base.
- Employer Social Security tax = Applicable covered wages × 6.2%, subject to the same wage base.
- Employee Medicare tax = Applicable Medicare wages × 1.45%.
- Employer Medicare tax = Applicable Medicare wages × 1.45%.
- Additional Medicare Tax = 0.9% on employee Medicare wages above the applicable filing-status threshold, with no matching employer share.

**Self-Employed Mode**
- Apply the correct net-earnings adjustment (generally 92.35%) before calculating self-employment tax.
- Calculate Social Security and Medicare components using applicable self-employment rates and wage limits.
- Apply Additional Medicare Tax where required.
- Distinguish self-employment tax liability from any deductible half of regular self-employment tax.
- Do not automatically treat gross business revenue as net self-employment earnings without a clear input assumption.

Requirements:
- Verify the exact 2026 Social Security wage base and all applicable thresholds.
- Account for filing-status differences in Additional Medicare Tax.
- Distinguish actual Additional Medicare Tax liability from employer withholding thresholds.
- Do not treat the employer share as a deduction from the employee's take-home pay.
- Handle zero income and wage-base limits correctly.
- Avoid double-counting employee/employer taxes.
- Prevent NaN, Infinity and invalid negative results.
- Preserve full numerical precision and apply appropriate tax rounding.
- Do not invent federal income tax or state payroll tax calculations.
