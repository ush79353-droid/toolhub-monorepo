## 3. Accurate Calculations

Use a transparent constant-driving-rate projection.

Let:
- Total term = T months.
- Remaining term = R months.
- Elapsed term = T − R.
- Current distance = D.
- Annual allowance = A distance units/year.
- Excess fee = F per distance unit.

Calculate:

- Estimated annual driving rate = D × 12 / Elapsed Term.
- Predicted total distance = D × T / Elapsed Term.
- Total allowed distance = A × T / 12.
- Predicted excess distance = max(0, Predicted Total Distance − Total Allowed Distance).
- Predicted lease-end charge = Predicted Excess Distance × F.
- Monthly savings required = Predicted Lease-end Charge / Remaining Term.

Requirements:
- Normalize all km/mi inputs and fee distance units before calculating.
- Validate positive total term and `0 < remaining term < total term` for the projection.
- If no lease time has elapsed, show an informative unavailable estimate rather than dividing by zero.
- Handle zero excess mileage, zero fee and valid zero current distance.
- Preserve full precision and round only for display.
- Clearly label the projection assumption: future driving continues at the historical average rate.
- Do not invent lease penalties, taxes or other charges.
