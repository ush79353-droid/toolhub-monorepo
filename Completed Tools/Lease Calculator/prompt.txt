## 3. Accurate Lease Calculations

Implement a standard fixed-rate lease model with an end-of-term residual/buyout value.

Let:
- P = Product value.
- D = Down payment.
- R = Residual value.
- n = Lease term in months.
- i = Annual interest rate / 1200.

Use the present-value equation:

`P − D = PMT × [1 − (1+i)^(-n)] / i + R / (1+i)^n`

Solve for monthly payment:

`PMT = [(P − D) − R/(1+i)^n] × i / [1 − (1+i)^(-n)]`

For zero interest:

`PMT = (P − D − R) / n`

Calculate:
- Total lease installments = PMT × n.
- Total payments = down payment + total lease installments.
- Total interest = total payments + residual value − product value.
- Total cost to own = total payments + residual buyout value.

Clearly distinguish lease installments from the optional buyout. Assume buyout occurs at lease end and is included only in total cost to own, not ordinary lease payments.

Requirements:
- Validate positive term and product value.
- Validate residual and down payment against product value.
- Handle 0% interest and zero residual.
- Prevent NaN, Infinity and negative payment amounts.
- Preserve full precision; round only for display.
- Do not invent taxes, insurance, registration fees or mileage penalties.
