## 3. Accurate Calculations

Use the Gordon Growth Dividend Discount Model.

- Retention ratio = 1 − Dividend payout ratio.
- Expected growth rate = Retention ratio × ROE.
- Expected dividend = Current dividend per share × (1 + Expected growth rate).
- Cost of equity = Risk-free rate + Beta × Market risk premium.
- Stock value = Expected dividend / (Cost of equity − Expected growth rate).

Use decimal rates internally.

Requirements:
- Treat dividend per share as the most recent dividend (D₀), with expected dividend as D₁.
- Clearly document the constant-growth assumption.
- Require cost of equity greater than expected growth rate.
- Handle zero/negative growth when mathematically valid.
- Validate payout ratio, dividend and rate inputs.
- Prevent NaN, Infinity and division by zero.
- Preserve full precision; round only for display.
- Do not invent unsupported multi-stage DDM modes or charts.
