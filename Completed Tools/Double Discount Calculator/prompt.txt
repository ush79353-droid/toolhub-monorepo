## 3. Accurate Calculations

Apply discounts **sequentially**, not by simply adding percentages.

- Final Price = Original Price × (1 − D1/100) × (1 − D2/100)
- Total Savings = Original Price − Final Price
- Effective Discount (%) = D1 + D2 − (D1 × D2 / 100)

Example: Two 20% discounts produce a **36% effective discount**, not 40%.

Requirements:
- Automatically recalculate results.
- Validate original price ≥ 0 and discounts between 0% and 100%.
- Support zero discounts and full 100% discounts.
- Prevent NaN, Infinity and negative final prices.
- Preserve full precision; round only for display.
- Do not invent additional discount levels or unsupported calculation modes.
